# About Company A

Company A was founded in March 2019 by Bob and Carol after they left their
previous employer to build what they believed the market was missing: a
context engine that treats organizational memory as a first-class product
surface. They started in a co-working space in Berlin with one laptop, a
whiteboard covered in graph diagrams, and a customer letter of intent from
Company B.

## The early days

Bob handled product and customer development. Carol — a former
infrastructure engineer at a payments company — built the first version of
the ingestion pipeline herself. The original prototype ran on a single
PostgreSQL box on a leased server in Frankfurt. It indexed roughly 12,000
documents for Company B over the first six months of the partnership and
proved, in Bob's words, "that the unsexy problem of remembering what your
own company knows is the real one worth solving."

Dave joined as the third engineer in late 2019. He came from a search
infrastructure background and rebuilt the retrieval layer over a single
quarter — what we now call the hybrid retriever. Grace joined in 2021 as
CTO when the team crossed twelve people and the architecture needed an
owner who could think in five-year horizons.

## What we believe

Three things have stayed constant since 2019:

1. **Context belongs to the user, not the model.** We design the system so
   that the same question asked by different people in the same company
   produces honest, not identical, answers — every fact is gated by who
   was supposed to know it.
2. **Bi-temporal facts.** Time is not a metadata column. When something
   was true in the world, and when our system believed it, are different
   axes. Both are first-class.
3. **No magic.** Every answer can be traced to its source episode. If
   we cannot explain why we said something, we did not earn the right to
   say it.

## Where we are now

As of 2026, Company A serves customers across financial services,
healthcare, and B2B software. Company B remains our longest-running
customer and an investor in our Series A. Company C joined in 2023 and
is now one of our largest deployments by document count.

The team is around 80 people, split across Berlin, London, and a remote
network across Europe and North America. We are profitable on a unit
basis, growing the customer base deliberately, and hiring slowly — by
design.

Bob is CEO. Carol is CFO. Grace is CTO. Dave leads the retrieval team.
