# CONFIDENTIAL — Master Services Agreement: Company A and Company B

**Effective date:** 2026-02-01
**Term:** 36 months, with two 12-month automatic renewals
**Document type:** Executed contract
**Counterparties:** Company A (provider) and Company B (customer)
**Distribution:** Legal counsel and named executives only. This document
contains binding commercial terms and is protected under both companies'
NDAs.

## 1. Commercial terms

**Annual contract value (year 1):** $2,840,000
**Annual contract value (year 2):** $3,120,000
**Annual contract value (year 3):** $3,420,000

Year 2 and year 3 prices reflect contractually agreed annual escalations
of 9.8%. Renewal years (4 and 5) will be priced at a 6.5% escalation,
or the European HICP plus 200 basis points, whichever is lower.

**Payment terms:** Annual prepay, net-30 from invoice. Invoiced on the
contract anniversary each year.

**Volume entitlements:**
- Up to 4,500 named users.
- Up to 25 million documents under index.
- Up to 80 million queries per year.

Overages are billed at $0.42 per 1,000 incremental queries and $0.018
per 1,000 incremental documents indexed beyond the entitlement, with a
20% overage allowance before billing kicks in.

## 2. Service level commitments

- **Uptime:** 99.9% measured monthly, with credits for breaches.
- **Query latency:** p95 under 350 milliseconds for queries against
  Company B's tenant.
- **Data residency:** All Company B data stored exclusively in the
  EU region, with no cross-region replication.
- **Support:** 24/5 with a 2-hour response SLA for severity-1
  incidents during Company B's market hours.

## 3. Pricing concessions

The following concessions were granted by Bob during the negotiation
and are NOT to be referenced in any other customer contract:

- **Year-1 ramp credit:** $340,000 credit applied against year-1
  invoice, reflecting Company B's role as our longest-running customer.
- **Migration services:** First 5,000 hours of professional services
  (estimated at $1.2M) provided at no incremental cost.
- **MFN clause (limited):** Company B is entitled to match terms
  offered to any other customer at the same or smaller scale, for a
  period of 24 months. This clause is unusual for Company A and was
  approved by Bob and the board chair.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with 60 days' notice
and an opportunity to cure. Company B may terminate for convenience
at the start of year 3 with 180 days' written notice, in which case
year-3 fees are waived but no refund is owed for year-2.

Company A's termination right is limited to material breach only.

## 5. Audit and regulatory

Company B may audit Company A's security controls once per contract
year, with 30 days' written notice. Audit findings classified as
"major" must be remediated within 90 days; "critical" findings within
30 days. The audit fees are borne by Company B.

Company A commits to deliver the regulatory-grade audit log export
capability (referenced publicly in the partnership announcement) no
later than 2026-12-31. Failure to deliver triggers a $250,000 service
credit.

## 6. Confidentiality

The terms of this agreement — including pricing, the year-1 ramp
credit, the MFN clause, and the volume entitlements — are mutually
confidential. Disclosure to anyone outside the named executives at
either party (including but not limited to other customers, press,
or analysts) requires written consent from both parties.

Bob (CEO, Company A) and Marta (head of research engineering at
Company B, signing under power of attorney from Company B's CEO)
signed this agreement on 2026-01-28.
