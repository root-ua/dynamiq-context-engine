# Halcyon Labs Q2 2026 Personnel Memo

**Effective:** 2026-05-21
**Author:** Daniel Cho (CTO, Halcyon Labs)
**Distribution:** Halcyon Labs all-hands

This memo captures three leadership transitions taking effect today and
announces a new strategic partnership.

## Leadership transitions effective 2026-05-21

### Product leadership

Effective 2026-05-21, **Marco Rivers replaces Lina Park as VP of
Product at Halcyon Labs**. Lina Park is beginning a six-month
sabbatical through 2026-12-31. Marco Rivers reports to Daniel Cho.

### Velocity dashboard ownership

**Aisha Khan now owns the Velocity dashboard at Halcyon Labs**, taking
over from Sven Halverson effective 2026-05-21. Sven Halverson is
transitioning to a new Staff Platform Engineer role on the platform
team and will no longer own Velocity.

Aisha Khan reports to Daniel Cho for the Velocity work.

### Engineering management

**Riya Bhat is promoted to Director of Engineering at Halcyon Labs**,
effective 2026-05-21. She continues to lead the Atlas Engineering team
at the director level. Tomás Pena is promoted to Principal Engineer
and now leads the Auth squad inside Atlas Engineering.

## New partnership: Project Beacon

Halcyon Labs has entered a strategic partnership with **Lighthouse
Compliance** on Project Beacon, a joint SOC 2 automation initiative.
The partnership was approved by Daniel Cho and Tatiana Volkov (Founder,
Lighthouse Compliance) at a kickoff meeting on 2026-05-21.

**Jorge Almeida** joins Halcyon Labs as Staff Engineer effective
2026-05-21 and leads engineering for Project Beacon.

**Maya Patel** joins Halcyon Labs as Product Manager effective
2026-05-21 and owns the Project Beacon roadmap.

Both Jorge Almeida and Maya Patel report to Daniel Cho.

Tatiana Volkov is the executive sponsor at Lighthouse Compliance and
the primary point of contact for the partnership.

## Schedule

The Project Beacon MVP ships on 2026-08-15. The first joint customer
pilot is scheduled for 2026-10-01. The partnership agreement runs
through 2027-12-31 with an option to renew.

## Action items

- Marco Rivers will publish the updated Halcyon product roadmap by
  2026-05-28.
- Aisha Khan will run a Velocity dashboard handover session with Sven

## Risks

The Beacon initiative depends on Lighthouse Compliance shipping their
audit-trail API by 2026-07-15. If that slips, the Beacon MVP date
slips with it. Tatiana Volkov flagged that Lighthouse is currently
hiring a head of engineering and the role is still open as of today.

The Velocity dashboard handover has a two-week risk window. Sven
Halverson and Aisha Khan have committed to daily standups during the
transition to mitigate.
