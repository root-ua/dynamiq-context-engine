# Company A and Company B announce expanded partnership

**Berlin, 2026-02-18** — Company A, the context engine platform, today
announced an expanded multi-year partnership with Company B, one of the
largest independent asset managers in the Nordics.

Under the expanded agreement, Company B will roll out Company A across
all of its analyst teams, replacing several internal tools used today
for institutional memory and document search. Company B was Company A's
first customer in 2019 and remains a strategic partner.

## What changes for Company B

The expanded deployment covers approximately 4,500 users across Company
B's research, portfolio management, and operations functions. Company B
plans to migrate roughly 18 million internal documents over the course
of 2026. The deployment will use Company A's enterprise edition with
private deployment in Company B's own cloud tenancy.

Marta, head of research engineering at Company B, said in a written
statement: "Company A is the only product we evaluated that took
permissioning seriously enough to deploy across a regulated firm without
us having to bolt anything on top. The team's bi-temporal model also maps
unusually cleanly to how research notes age in our business — what was
true a quarter ago is its own kind of truth, separate from what we
believe today."

Bob, CEO of Company A, said the partnership reflects a multi-year
relationship: "Company B took a chance on us in 2019. The fact that
they're scaling Company A across the whole research org seven years
later is the kind of validation you cannot manufacture."

## Roadmap commitments

As part of the expanded agreement, Company A has committed to ship the
following capabilities by the end of 2026:

- Native connectors for two of Company B's internal document stores.
- A regulatory-grade audit log export, suitable for Company B's
  compliance reviews.
- A jurisdiction-aware data residency option for Company B's London
  office.

The companies declined to disclose financial terms.

## About Company A

Company A is a context engine for organizational memory, founded in 2019
by Bob and Carol. The company is headquartered in Berlin and serves
customers across financial services, healthcare, and software.

## About Company B

Company B is an independent asset manager headquartered in Stockholm,
serving institutional clients across the Nordics and continental Europe.
