# Bob — CEO

**Role:** Chief Executive Officer
**Joined:** March 2019 (co-founder)
**Based in:** Berlin
**Pronouns:** he/him
**Slack:** @bob
**Calendar:** bob@company-a.example

## What Bob works on

Bob owns product direction, customer relationships, and fundraising. He
runs the weekly leadership sync on Mondays at 09:00 CET and the monthly
all-hands. For anything related to commercial strategy, contracts, or
partnerships, Bob is the right person to ask first.

For technical or architectural questions, route to Grace or Dave.
For finance, hiring budgets, or vendor questions, route to Carol.

## How Bob likes to work

- Async-first. Bob prefers Slack threads with context over surprise
  meetings. If you need a 30-minute slot, send a one-paragraph agenda
  first.
- Bob blocks deep work on Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Avoid scheduling
  these unless it is urgent.
- Bob is on a four-week cadence with each customer success owner — see
  the customer-owner rotation page for the schedule.

## Background

Bob co-founded Company A with Carol in March 2019. Before Company A, he
spent six years at a developer tools company in product management, where
he and Carol worked together on the search team. He has a degree in
linguistics from a university in the Netherlands and writes occasionally
about graph systems on the company engineering blog.
